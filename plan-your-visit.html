Everything practical in one place: how to reach us, when to come, what to bring, and the questions families usually ask before they write.

The short versionFifty kilometres from Chiang Mai, about an hour. Four rooms. Everything included. Book by email.

Getting here

The farm sits in a valley fifty kilometres from Chiang Mai, surrounded by national park. The drive takes about an hour. There are no paved roads to the main gate, and the last stretch runs through a river, which is why most guests arrive in the farm truck.

We will collect you from anywhere in the city, the international airport, the railway station or the bus station. The transfer is 1,200 THB each way for the farm truck, or 1,400 THB for a minibus if the truck is busy or there are more than four of you. That is for the vehicle, not per person.

If you are driving yourself, tell us and we will explain the last part of the route.

When to come

We are up in the hills, so it is cooler here than in the city all year round, and properly cold on winter nights.

The seasons, roughly

Cool and dry, November to February
Busiest, and lovely
Hot, March to May
Quiet mornings, slow afternoons
Green season, June to October
Rain, and the valley at its greenest

During the rains we are quite isolated, which some guests come for deliberately.

Minimum stays

In November, December and January we ask for at least three nights. At other times a single night is possible, though there is a small supplement for one and two night stays — the details are on the rooms and rates page.

What to bring

  • Shoes you don't mind getting muddy.
  • Something warm for the evenings between November and February. It gets colder here than people expect.
  • Insect repellent, though we will have some if you forget.
  • A torch. There are no streetlights.
  • Swimming things, for the hot springs and the waterfalls.
  • Any medicine you take regularly. The nearest pharmacy is not close.
To check with Ae: mosquitoes and what he does about them, whether there are nets, and where the nearest clinic and pharmacy are. Parents ask all three, usually by email, and answering here saves him the reply.

You won't need much moneyEverything is included. Bring cash for a massage, a day out, or a jar of honey to take home.

Questions people ask

Is the farm suitable for young children?

Yes, and most of our guests are families. The Sunset Room and the Bunk Room next door work well for parents and children, cots are free, and camping on the farm costs nothing. Children spend their days feeding animals, walking to the waterfalls and making krathongs.

What will my children eat?

Everything is cooked here. If your children won't eat Thai food, tell Ae before you arrive and he will cook something they will.

By email, direct with Ae. He will send you an exact price for your family. [Confirm the deposit and payment arrangement, then state it here.]

Do you need a car?

No. Ae collects guests from Chiang Mai and drives them himself to the waterfalls, the hot springs, the villages and back into the city.

Can we come for one night?

Outside November to January, yes, with a small supplement for the extra laundry and staff. In November, December and January we ask for three nights.
