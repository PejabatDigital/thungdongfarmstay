What we ask for your stay

We are not a resort, nor a hotel, nor a guest house. We are a country farm in the hills, and what we ask from our guests goes towards the farm and the care of our animals. We keep it as low as we can.

There is nowhere to eat within twenty kilometres of the farm, so everything is included: a room, three meals a day, afternoon tea and refreshments, and the farm activities. The prices below are per night for two guests, and they are for guidance. Write to me with your dates and who is coming, and I'll send you an exact figure.

Everything includedRoom, three meals, afternoon tea, refreshments and farm activities. You won't need your wallet unless you'd like a massage or a trip out.

The four rooms

The Farm House

4,200 / 4,700 THBlow season / high season

This was the chili pickers' quarters when we bought the farm. We extended it, converted it, and refurbished it. Only the bedrooms, bathroom and kitchen have walls. The rest is open to the air.

Sleeping: one double bedroom with a lounge, and one single bedroom, sharing a rustic bathroom. We can add more beds.

There is an open lounge and dining area, a kitchen, and a Chinese day-bed verandah with loungers. A staircase at the far end now connects the Garden Room, the Thai kitchen and another bathroom. There is a satellite television if you want to keep an eye on the outside world. It is built entirely of wood, and it is the most comfortable place on the farm for a family that wants room to spread out.

The Sunset Room and The Bunk Room

3,700 + 2,500 THBthe family annex

These two sit side by side in converted outbuildings, sharing one long verandah that faces west over the pasture and catches the late sun. Together they make a private, self-contained corner of the farm. The parents take the Sunset Room, the children take the Bunk Room next door.

The Sunset Room — 3,700 low, 4,200 high. A kitchenette, a private garden, and a wide sala that is perfect for eating outside in the warmer months. Views across the lower field, and the best sunsets on the farm.

The Bunk Room — 2,500 low, 2,800 high. Compact, sleeps three, with one bunk and a single bed and a big bathroom. It is usually the children's favourite.

The Bunk Room is only available together with the Sunset Room.

The Garden Room

2,900 / 3,400 THBlow season / high season

A small house near the Thai kitchen and the open fire we light in the winter. One double bed, and a fold-away bed if you need one. A curtain divides it into two sleeping areas. The ceiling planks were cut by hand from a teak tree that fell on the farm. There is a separate wash room and shower room.

Sleeping: right for two, or for two with one or two small children.

Camping on the farm

No chargewith a room booked

If you'd like to be closer to the outdoors, we'll set up a tent for you, with sleeping bags, mattresses and everything else you need. Near the farm house, by the lake, or down in the jungle valley where there isn't another building in sight and yet it's a five minute walk back for breakfast.

There is no charge for it, and children love it. Camping is for guests who have also booked a room.

Getting here

The farm is fifty kilometres from Chiang Mai and the drive takes about an hour. We will collect you from the city, the airport or the railway station.

The price is for the vehicle, not per person.
