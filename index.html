Come and stay on a real farm in the hills above Chiang Mai

A family farm in a quiet valley, surrounded by national park, an hour from the city. A room, three meals a day, and as much or as little farm life as you like.

There are four rooms. That's all.

Sawasdee krap. My name is Ae

Sixteen years ago I bought a small piece of land in the hills fifty kilometres from Chiang Mai, with a river running through it and jungle all around. I had no farming experience at all. I have spent every year since learning it the slow way.

I live here and I look after every part of it. I feed the animals, I cook for our guests, I drive them to the waterfalls, and I sit down with them at dinner. There is no reception desk. There is just me, my family, and the farm.

We are not a resort, nor a hotel, nor a guest house. We are a country farm high in the hills of northern Thailand. The farm may not be as pristine as a five star resort, but we try our very best to make our guests welcome and comfortable.

How the farm came to be

A day here

Feed the animals before breakfast. Walk up the valley to the waterfalls, where the fish jump the falls if you bring food for them. Learn to cook a Thai dish for lunch. Rest through the heat of the afternoon. Soak in the hot springs. After dinner, go out and look for fireflies.

Or do none of it. Some guests spend three days barely leaving the verandah, and that is a perfectly good way to use the farm.

Everything is included, and bring the children

There are no restaurants within twenty kilometres of the farm, so your stay covers everything: a room, three meals a day, afternoon tea and refreshments, and the farm activities. You won't need your wallet unless you'd like a massage or a trip out.

Children are very welcome. The Sunset Room sits next door to the Bunk Room, which sleeps three and is where the children usually end up, sharing one long verandah. A cot costs nothing. Camping on the farm costs nothing either, and we'll set the tent up near the farm house, by the lake, or down in the jungle valley.
